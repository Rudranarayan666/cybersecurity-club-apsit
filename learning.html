Entropy & Passwords

A password's strength is measured by its entropy—how difficult it is for an attacker to guess using brute-force or dictionary attacks. Length is often more important than complexity.

Best Practices

Use long passphrases (e.g., `CorrectHorseBatteryStaple`) rather than short, complex passwords (e.g., `P@$$w0rd!`). Consider a Password Manager.
